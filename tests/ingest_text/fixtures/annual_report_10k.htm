Item 1. Business.
Bigbank Corp is a financial holding company. It provides consumer banking, commercial banking and asset management services to clients in the United States and abroad through its bank subsidiaries.
Supervision and regulation
Bigbank is subject to regulation by the Federal Reserve, the OCC and the FDIC. These regulators set capital and liquidity requirements and examine the bank regularly.
Item 1A. Risk Factors.
Bigbank's businesses are highly regulated, and changes in regulation can reduce its earnings.
New capital rules could require Bigbank to hold more capital, which would reduce its return on equity and limit its ability to return capital to shareholders.
Bigbank can be harmed by adverse changes in interest rates.
A sudden fall in interest rates would reduce net interest income because loan yields would reprice faster than deposit costs.

Management’s discussion and analysis
The following is Management’s discussion and analysis of the financial condition and results of operations of Bigbank for the year ended December 31, 2024.
EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW
Bigbank reported net income of $9.1 billion in 2024, up 8% from 2023, on record net revenue of $40.2 billion. Net interest income rose 3% on higher loan balances.
MARKET RISK MANAGEMENT
Market risk is the risk of loss from changes in market prices and rates. Bigbank measures market risk with value-at-risk and stress tests and sets limits for each business.
Management’s report on internal control over financial reporting
Management of Bigbank is responsible for establishing and maintaining adequate internal control over financial reporting, and it concluded that this control was effective as of December 31, 2024.
