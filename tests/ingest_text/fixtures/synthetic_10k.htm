ITEM 1. BUSINESS
Our Company
Acme Corp designs and sells widgets to industrial customers around the world. Founded in 1950, the company is headquartered in Springfield.
Our widgets are used in factories, warehouses and data centers, and we sell them through direct sales and a network of

Competition
The widget market is highly competitive. Our main competitors are Globex and Initech, which compete on price and delivery times.
Our competitive advantages include quality, a broad catalog and long customer relationships.
•Quality: our widgets have the lowest return rate in the industry.
•Catalog: we offer more than 5,000 widget models.
•Relationships: our top customers have bought from us for over 20 years.
Human Capital
As of December 31, 2024, we had approximately 12,000 employees, of whom 4,000 work in research and development.

Item 1A. Risk Factors
Investing in our securities involves risk. You should carefully consider the risks described below before making an investment decision.
Risks Related to Our Business
Demand for widgets is cyclical and a downturn could reduce our revenue.
Our revenue depends on capital spending by industrial customers. In past downturns our revenue fell by as much as 30 percent.
We depend on a small number of suppliers for steel.
Three suppliers provide most of our steel. A disruption at any of them could delay our shipments.

Item 7A. Quantitative and Qualitative Disclosures About Market Risk
Interest Rate Risk
We hold $500 million of fixed-rate investments. A 100 basis point increase in interest rates would decrease their fair value by about $5 million.
Foreign Exchange Risk
About a third of our revenue is in currencies other than the U.S. dollar, mainly the euro and the yen. We do not hedge this exposure.
